Summary Report – Online Learner Purchase Behaviour Analysis

Key Insights from the Analysis

1.
Course Demand: Courses related to Database and Programming categories show strong purchase activity, indicating high demand for technical skills.
2.
Top Purchased Courses: A small number of courses contribute to the majority of purchases, showing that certain courses are significantly more popular than others.
3.
Revenue Distribution: Total revenue varies across courses and categories depending on course price and purchase quantity.
4.
Learner Engagement: Some learners purchased multiple courses and across different categories, suggesting higher engagement and interest in skill diversification.
5.
Category Performance: Categories such as Data Visualization, Programming, and Database contribute noticeably to platform revenue through course purchases.
6.
Cross-Category Learning: Several learners enrolled in courses from more than one category, indicating opportunities for cross-selling related courses.
7.
Unpurchased Courses: A few courses have no purchase records, indicating potential issues with visibility, pricing, or learner interest.

Recommendations

•
Focus marketing efforts on high-performing course categories such as programming and data-related skills.
•
Introduce course bundles or learning paths to encourage learners to purchase multiple courses.
•
Promote underperforming courses through discounts or featured placement on the platform.
•
Provide personalized course recommendations to learners who have already made purchases.
